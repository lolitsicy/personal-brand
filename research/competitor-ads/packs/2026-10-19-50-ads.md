# Metabolic Reset: 50-Ad Deck v1

Built 19 Oct 2026. Phase: waitlist. CTA button: **Sign up** (lead form) or **Learn more**. Copy CTA: "join the waitlist".
Structures: **R** = rhode (object-first, lowercase, few words). **A** = Arrae (hook, stacked benefit lines, ↓ CTA).
Formats: **4:5** = Instagram post 1080×1350 (feed). **9:16** = Story/Reel 1080×1920. Resize in Canva for the other placement.
Images: every Canva template is text-only. Drop in the JCHQ shot named in "Image" (set names from the Shoot No. 1 deck).

Footer for every ad's primary text:
> Always read the label and follow the directions for use. If symptoms persist, see your healthcare professional. Dietary supplements are not a replacement for a balanced diet.

**[CONFIRM]** = true only if the fact is confirmed. **[HOLD]** = do not run until ingredients are public.

## Meta structure (campaigns → ad sets → ads)

These are 50 **ads**, not 50 ad sets. Fifty ad sets on a waitlist budget would split spend so thin that none exit learning. Run 5 campaigns, 1–2 broad ad sets each, ads inside.
