# Reel filming scripts: Metabolic Reset organic calendar

Built 23 Oct 2026 from `posts.csv` (every row where format = REEL; 29 Reels). Each Reel is cross-posted to TikTok. Covers already exist in Canva; these scripts are what gets filmed. The batch-by-set shot list is `reel-shotlist.csv`.

## How to use

- **On-screen text** is exact wording, lowercase, burned in. Max about 6 words per card where possible. `/` = next card or line.
- **Sets** use the Shoot No. 1 names: Chrome Platter, Under the Glass, Gradient Backdrop, Product & Essentials (vanity/chrome tray), Inside the bag, capsules + tweezers, capsules on mirror. Anything else is labelled Off-set, BTS, Pre-shoot or Canva.
- **Before 23 Nov** (product reveal) no bottle appears on screen. Never mock up or render a bottle. Real packshots arrive ~22 Nov.
- **Creator and founder lines are suggested wording only.** They rewrite in their own words, then the script is approved before filming.
- **Audio:** the brand account must use licensed audio (Meta Sound Collection / TikTok Commercial Music Library). Audio notes describe a vibe, not a track.
- **Safe zone (both platforms):** keep text in the centre band, roughly 15% clear at the top, 25% at the bottom (caption, handle) and 15% on the right (buttons). TikTok notes below cover what else changes.
- Shoot 9:16, 1080x1920, 4K if possible. Grade: white, ice blue, chrome. No warm neutrals, no neon.

## Compliance reminder (NZ/AU)

- Structure/function only: "formulated to support metabolism, blood sugar balance and gut health." Never cure, treat, prevent, detox, burn fat, weight loss, or any outcome or timeframe.
- No before/after bodies, measuring tapes, scales, stomach pinching, pill-spill, or identifiable competitor products.
- No invented testimonials, stats, doses, prices or ingredients. Placeholders stay until confirmed: [dose], [price], [ingredient 4], [CONFIRM]. A Reel with a placeholder on screen does not post until it is filled.
- CTA: before 3 Dec "join the waitlist"; from 3 Dec "shop now".
- Creators: no results, energy or body claims. Paid partnership label plus #ad when paid or gifted.
- Any Reel that names the product's function carries the footer in its caption: *Always read the label and follow the directions for use. If symptoms persist, see your healthcare professional. Dietary supplements are not a replacement for a balanced diet.*
