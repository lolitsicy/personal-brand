# How to Create a Luxury Watch Commercial: Rolex Panda Daytona

*AI Product Video Guides / How to Create a Luxury Watch Commercial: Rolex Panda Daytona*

Recreate a cinematic, photorealistic luxury watch commercial in two steps: one character sheet, one Seedance 2.0 video. No filming, no studio, no crew. This guide recreates a low-key black-background dive watch commercial shot for shot, swapping in the Rolex Cosmograph Daytona "Panda" and its own hardware (Oystersteel case, black ceramic tachymeter bezel, white lacquer dial, red "DAYTONA" script).

## Step 1: Generate a Product Character Sheet

Take a few clean photos of your watch, or start from the brand's press images, and turn them into a multi-angle character sheet so every later shot stays consistent.

Go to Higgsfield:

1. Select model: GPT Image 2
2. Set aspect ratio to 16:9
3. Set quality to 2K or 4K
4. Add product images (front-on hero, three-quarter, caseback, lateral profile)
5. Paste the prompt below

> Generate a professional product character sheet and technical blueprint for cross-shot continuity. Subject: Rolex Cosmograph Daytona "Panda", an Oystersteel chronograph wristwatch with a white lacquer dial, three black recessed subdials, a black ceramic tachymeter bezel and a brushed-and-polished Oyster bracelet. All 5 views depict the exact same single product instance with identical colors, proportions, materials and details, rendered at consistent relative scale. Match the attached reference images precisely in every view. Format: A single horizontal 16:9 layout on light gray paper (#DDDDDD). The central band contains 5 equal-width product views in one horizontal row, each isolated on a solid black backdrop (#000000). Product only in every view: no hands, props, packaging or stray reflections. The 5 views:
> 1. "3/4 L": three-quarter view from the front left, case angled to catch the light along the polished lug flanks
> 2. "HERO": straight-on frontal view of the dial, watch upright, bracelet extending above and below
> 3. "SIDE": clean lateral profile showing the case flank, the crown and two screw-down chronograph pushers, and the thickness of the ceramic bezel
> 4. "BACK": direct rear view showing the closed Oystersteel caseback, the fluted caseback rim, and the Oysterlock clasp with the engraved crown on the bracelet
> 5. "MACRO": extreme close-up of the white lacquer dial where the red "DAYTONA" script sits above the black running-seconds subdial, showing the applied index edges and the subdial's snailed texture
>
> Document chrome and labels:
> - Above each view, a clean bold monospace label matching its angle: "3/4 L", "HERO", "SIDE", "BACK", "MACRO".
> - Top margin header: "BRAND: ROLEX" on the top-left, "CHARACTER SHEET v1" in the top-center, "CATEGORY: LUXURY CHRONOGRAPH WATCH" on the top-right.
> - Below the image row, four technical data blocks with generous spacing and zero text overlap:
>   1. "COLOR PALETTE": 4 swatches of the product's dominant colors, each labeled with a hex code: white lacquer dial #F5F5F3, black ceramic bezel and subdials #0B0B0B, Oystersteel #B9BDC0, Daytona red #C8102E.

Hyper-detailed, photorealistic. Luxury chronograph watch editorial.

This single sheet becomes your one reference image for every shot in the video. Swap "Rolex Cosmograph Daytona" and its case description for any product to reuse this for a different piece.

## Step 2: Generate the Seedance 2.0 Video

Go to Higgsfield and set up your generation:

1. Select model: Seedance 2.0
2. Set duration to the longest available option (the cut is timed to 15 seconds)
3. Set aspect ratio to 16:9
4. Set quality to 720 or 1080p
5. Upload the character sheet from Step 1 as the only attachment, referenced as @sheet
6. Paste the prompt below
7. Hit Generate

> Luxury watch commercial, 15 seconds, 8 hard-cut shots, no transitions, pure black background throughout. The product is the Rolex Cosmograph Daytona "Panda" shown in @sheet. Use @sheet as the single all-around reference and absolute visual truth for the product in every shot: identical Oystersteel case and Oyster bracelet, black ceramic tachymeter bezel with white numerals, white lacquer dial with three black subdials and red "DAYTONA" script. Build each angle from the matching view in @sheet. Keep the product design, colorway and hardware finish unchanged across all shots. Do not reproduce the sheet layout, labels, gray paper or data blocks: extract only the product. Extreme low-key lighting, one hard key plus rim separation, no fill, deep black falloff.
>
> [00:00] Near-total darkness. Only the top curve of the steel case and the first bracelet links catch a hard rim light from directly above, a thin silver crescent against black. Camera pushes in slowly.
> [00:01.6] Macro on the right flank, built from the SIDE view. Camera orbits slowly right as the fluted crown and two screw-down pushers rotate into a hard raking key from the left.
> [00:03.4] Macro across the Oyster bracelet. Camera slides laterally left to right while raking light sweeps along the links, brushed and polished surfaces flaring in sequence.
> [00:04.7] The bracelet arcs vertically through frame. The product rotates slowly, the Oysterlock clasp swinging through a soft strip light.
> [00:06.5] The dial emerges from darkness, built from the 3/4 L view. The product rotates its face toward camera at three-quarter from above-left, white lacquer dial and black bezel resolving into a broad soft key.

> Cinematic, photorealistic, luxury chronograph campaign. Monochrome palette of black, Oystersteel silver and white lacquer, with a single red accent. No distortion. Maintain product proportions. Steady motion, muted colors.

Wait a couple of minutes. Seedance sometimes ignores the requested aspect ratio, and it will not always reproduce an unusual angle like the extreme low lateral profile literally. Check your actual output before publishing.

## Key Rules That Prevent Bad Results

- **Strip the document chrome:** when the character sheet is your only attachment, tell the model explicitly to ignore the gray paper, the monospace labels and the data blocks. Otherwise Seedance will pull that layout into the video.
- **Lock the text explicitly:** name every wordmark in the Seedance prompt (the red "DAYTONA" script, the tachymeter numerals, "UNITS PER HOUR") and state that none of it may be altered, misspelled, or invented. A single reference image gives the model less to work with than a full storyboard, so text is the first thing to drift.
- **Fidelity over reinvention:** this is a shot-for-shot recreation, so match the reference's shot count, per-shot timing, angles, camera moves, lighting, and hard-cut style. Swap only the product, keep the black low-key setting.
- **Point each shot at a sheet view:** naming the SIDE, 3/4 L or HERO view inside a timestamped beat keeps the geometry honest across eight different angles.
- **Watch the tonality flip:** the reference watch had a dark blue dial glowing out of black. A white panda dial inverts that relationship and reads far brighter, so the reveal and macro beats will feel harder and whiter than the original.
- **Hard cuts only, one primary camera move per shot:** keeps the fast beats readable across the 15-second cut.
