# Copy Structures

Reusable structures distilled from live competitor ads. Source pack: research/competitor-ads/packs/2026-10-19-full-ads.md

## The two house structures

What we took from each brand's live ads, and what we refused.

**rhode structure** (concepts 01, 02, 05, 06, 08, 09)
1. Headline = the product name or a two-word idea, lowercase. ("peptide lip tint", "limited edition")
2. Primary text opens with "meet ___." or the name, then one sensory or object line.
3. One line on where it lives in her day.
4. CTA. Nothing else.
Taken: lowercase, object-first, very few words, launch-in-waves cadence.
Refused: nothing. The rhode structure carries no claims, so it maps cleanly. We add the regulatory footer rhode never needs.

**Arrae structure** (concepts 03, 04, 07)
1. Hook headline in sentence case, often first person or "POV".
2. Primary text: problem in one line, "here's what's different", 3–4 stacked benefit lines with a glyph, CTA with ↓.
3. The same headline held constant across many creatives and creators.
Taken: POV hooks, stacked benefit lines, one headline across many faces.
Refused: "#1 secret" and other unqualified superlatives, "obsessed" in brand voice (creators only), results language, and emoji in every line. One glyph style per ad.

Note: the Ad Library connector only gave us headlines. The body-copy structures above also draw on how these brands typically write. Open the linked ads to check before treating them as exact.
